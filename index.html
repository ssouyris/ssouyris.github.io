Sebastián Souyris

Assistant Professor of Supply Chain and Analytics
Lally School of Management, Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute (RPI)

My research addresses challenges and the means of achieving environmental and human sustainability, combining data-driven optimization, machine learning, and artificial intelligence. I have developed analytic solutions for sustainability, energy, healthcare, logistics, media, and sports, with work published in Operations Research, Production and Operations Management, the European Journal of Operational Research, and the INFORMS Journal on Applied Analytics.

I am an INFORMS Franz Edelman Laureate, a finalist of the EURO Excellence in Practice Award and of the INFORMS Innovative Applications in Analytics Award, and a prizewinner of the INFORMS Revenue Management and Pricing Practice Award.
